HR administrators: match and approve employee profiles, manage attendance sessions and view authorized attendance and reports. The initial designated administrator is hr@shikshavertex.in.

Attendance viewers: view authorized all-employee attendance records. Viewer access alone does not allow employee approvals, attendance changes or movement-history access.

## Employees: first-time setup

### Create or sign in to your account

1. Open the app and choose First-time registration. Enter your own work email and choose an app password. If you already have an account, choose Sign in instead.

2. Open the verification email and follow its link. Return to the app and tap I have verified my email. Check Spam/Junk if the message is missing; use Resend verification email after its cooldown ends.

3. If approval is requested, enter your full employee name and tap Request HR approval. Wait for HR to match your account with your employee profile. Tap Check approval status after HR reviews it.

4. If the request is rejected, read HR's reason and contact HR to correct the information. If account recovery is required, contact HR/your administrator; avoid creating a second account to work around it.

5. On My attendance, tap Enroll phone passkey when offered. Complete Android's fingerprint, face or device-PIN prompt using your own phone.

### Remember your credentials

Your app password is the password chosen during registration. Your device PIN is used only by Android's phone-verification prompt. The app does not collect fingerprint or face templates.

Use Show password when needed. If the app password is forgotten, tap Reset password, enter your registered email and follow the email instructions.

> Use your own email and phone. The app keeps an account binding for its local profile; signing out retains that binding. This is not a permanent hardware lock. Clearing app storage or reinstalling can require account/passkey setup or recovery again.

## Employees: mark daily attendance

1. Arrive at the venue, connect your phone to Shikshavertex_5G and enable precise Location in Android settings. Normal internet access is required.

2. Open My attendance. Tap Check office readiness and grant precise-location permission when prompted. The app checks while this dashboard is visible; this does not mark attendance or start daily location sharing.

3. Look for Venue GPS Ready and Connected to Shikshavertex_5G. Unknown, inaccurate or expired readings are not confirmation. These indicators help you prepare; the app checks again at submission.

4. Choose today's open attendance session. If there is no open session, ask HR to open it, then tap Refresh attendance.

5. Read and select the attendance statement. Tap Verify Phone & Mark Attendance and complete the Android fingerprint, face or device-PIN prompt.

6. Wait while Verifying Office Location appears. Real checks may take longer than the brief animation. Success appears only after the service confirms a saved record.

7. Look for Attendance Marked! and your saved check-in time. Verified at Office appears only when the saved record contains the required verification evidence. Your first timestamp is preserved if a duplicate request occurs.

### If check-in does not succeed

Outside-office warning: move inside and tap Re-verify Office Location. Connection failure: connect to Shikshavertex_5G through Android network settings, then retry. Unavailable GPS: allow precise location, enable Location and obtain a fresh accurate reading.

> If the app says Attendance status could not be confirmed, tap Refresh attendance before retrying. The request may have reached the server. Check your history/receipt; do not assume the attendance was rejected.

## Employees: sharing and personal records

### Optional workday location sharing

1. Open Workday location sharing - show details. Read what is collected and how it is used.

2. Give explicit consent, then tap Start location sharing for today during 10:30 AM-8:30 PM IST. Allow the location and notification permissions requested by Android.

3. Check the actual sharing status and Android foreground notification. Starting, active, stopped and unconfirmed states are different; tapping Start alone does not prove that sharing is active.

4. To stop collection, tap Stop Sharing in the app or use the tracking notification's stop control. Check the displayed local-stop and server-confirmation status. Stop remains available when you leave the venue.

5. Tap View today's movement report to review estimated venue/away time, departures, returns and approximate stops. Collection ends with the workday; a later day requires consent again.

> Opening the app and marking attendance do not automatically start daily location sharing. Android, connectivity and permission changes can interrupt delivery. Unknown report intervals must not be treated as confirmed travel or time at the office.

### Your attendance history

Your Android dashboard shows the saved receipt for the selected session. For the calendar and sortable check-in table, open the web portal, sign in and use My Attendance History. Select the relevant period and sort the check-in table as needed.

Keep a record of the selected session, date and displayed receipt time when asking HR about a discrepancy. Do not share passwords, device PINs or unrelated personal location data.

## HR: approvals and daily controls

### Sign in as the administrator

1. Use your designated HR administrator account, verify its email and sign in. If HR controls are missing, ask the application administrator to check the role. A job title or attendance-viewer account alone does not grant HR access.

### Approve an employee

1. On Android, open HR controls and the pending approval requests. On a computer, use the employee directory and approvals screen. Refresh to load current requests.

2. Review the verified work email and full name. Explicitly select the correct roster profile; compare employee name, employee ID, department and team. Do not infer an email address from a similar name.

3. Tick the profile-matching confirmation and choose Approve employee. Tell the employee to tap Check approval status. If the right profile is unavailable or ambiguous, ask the administrator to correct the roster before approving.

4. For an incorrect request, enter a clear reason and use Reject request. For account-recovery or revoked-binding cases, follow the administrator's recovery process rather than approving a different identity.

### Open and close attendance

1. On Android HR controls, tap Open today's attendance. This creates/reuses today's daily attendance session. Employees then refresh their dashboards.

2. On the web HR session controls screen, use Create attendance event when a separate session is needed. Enter its title and date, choose its mandatory setting and save. New sessions start closed; choose Open check-in when ready.

3. Use Close today's attendance or Close check-in when the session ends. Closing a session does not delete saved attendance. HR can manage sessions and approvals from anywhere.

> Browser manual attendance is unavailable under the required office-connection policy. Employees should complete their own Android check-in. Historical manual exceptions remain explicitly labelled and audited.

## HR & viewers: reports and troubleshooting

### View and export attendance

1. Open Everyone's attendance (All attendance on the Android dashboard). Choose the date and optional attendance session, then tap Refresh attendance records. Check the loaded date and last-refreshed time.

2. Search by employee and apply the available department, team and attendance-status filters. Not marked yet means there is no matching saved check-in in the selected scope; it is not an HR-confirmed absence or leave decision.

3. On the web, open Export attendance records by date range. Choose Export from and Export through, covering no more than 31 inclusive days and no future date. Enter an exact department name if needed, or leave it blank.

4. Select Export attendance records (CSV), then open the downloaded file in Excel or another spreadsheet app. This range export uses its own dates and department field; daily search/team/status/session filters do not narrow that file.

Movement reports are separate from attendance exports. Authorized HR users can choose an employee/date where permitted. Treat venue time, away time and stops as estimates and retain unknown gaps; they are not automatic payroll or disciplinary determinations.

### Common problems

Password rejected: use Reset password for the existing work email. Do not use an email mailbox password, device PIN or signing password as a substitute for your app password.

No open session: HR opens today's session; the employee refreshes. Missing employee in reports: check the date/session, current filters and approval/profile matching, then refresh.

Installation fails: use the signed attendance.apk, keep its .apk extension and install it on Android. An unsigned APK or setup ZIP is not an employee installer. For updates, keep the existing app installed when its signing identity matches.

Service unavailable: check internet and retry after a short wait; the hosted service may need time to wake. For an unconfirmed submission, refresh records before making another attempt.

> Contact HR at hr@shikshavertex.in. Include your work email, phone model/Android version, session/date, app version and exact error. Share only the relevant screenshot; hide unrelated employee or location data.
